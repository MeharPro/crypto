# Ultimate iOS Trading Bot

This project is an ambitious endeavor to create a powerful, AI-driven trading bot for iOS. The bot is designed to be a smart, automated trading assistant capable of trading a wide variety of assets (stocks, crypto, commodities, etc.) through brokerage APIs.

## Vision

The core vision is to create a tool that can turn a small investment into significant returns by leveraging artificial intelligence to analyze market trends and make lightning-fast trading decisions. The bot will scrape the web for news, social media sentiment, and other data to find trading opportunities, all while being controlled through a simple and intuitive iOS interface.

## Core Features

*   **Multi-Asset Trading**: Connects to various brokerage and exchange APIs to trade a wide range of assets.
*   **User-Friendly Controls**: An intuitive iOS app with sliders for risk level and target ROI, and an input for the investment amount.
*   **AI-Powered Trend Analysis**: The bot will scrape news, social media, and other web sources to identify trading opportunities.
*   **High-Frequency Trading Engine**: A powerful backend that can execute trades in milliseconds.
*   **Advanced Risk Management**: The bot will use sophisticated risk management techniques to protect the user's capital.
*   **Transparent Decision Making**: The bot will provide clear explanations for its trading decisions.

This project is guided by the comprehensive research document "Ultimate iOS Trading Bot – Vision, Features & Feasibility," which outlines the full feature set, technical considerations, and compliance requirements.
